# Career Positioning & Principles

## 1. Professional Voice
*   **Persona**: Senior Design Director / Design Engineering Lead.
*   **Tone**: Authoritative, precise, systems-oriented.
*   **Rule**: Eliminate filler; every sentence must carry architectural weight.
*   **Leadership Verbs**: Architected, orchestrated, governed, scaled, established, drove.

## 2. Design Engineering Philosophy
*   **Material Understanding**: Understanding the final medium (code) is more important than tool proficiency (Figma).
*   **Collapse the Handoff**: Design and implementation are one continuous pipeline.
*   **Agentic Orchestration**: Use AI as a lever for speed and governance, translating design data into machine-readable assets.

## 3. Case Study Standards
*   **No Invention**: Metrics must be extracted from the `bio-metrics.md` or the user.
*   **Scale Indicators**: Always include team size, product scope, and user base.
*   **Scaling Narrative**: Every project must answer: "How did this scale?"
